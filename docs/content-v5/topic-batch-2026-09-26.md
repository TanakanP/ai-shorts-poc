Those are useful reruns under the stated rule, but their first frames need a clearer physical object and quicker visible contradiction.

This export is observational. Videos have different publication dates and exposure time, so ranking by views is a selection rule, not proof that a topic caused more reach. Stayed to watch and average percentage viewed are separate YouTube measures; neither is interchangeable with Views or Engaged views. The data do not isolate posting time, packaging, audience, or distribution effects. Re-running a topic will test a revised treatment, not reproduce its earlier outcome. ## Editorial direction for the 23 new topics

The top performers point toward **visible everyday design rules**: a painted runway number, sign shape, road sensor, mail-truck position, and fuel-gauge arrow. The new briefs expand that pattern into airport cues, roadside engineering, household products, mail/barcode systems, cash security, and simple physical phenomena. Each has (1) a recognizable first-frame object, (2) a specific mechanism, (3) a distinct second reveal, and (4) primary-source links and factual boundaries inside its JSON brief. Examples include the FAA’s [PAPI light code](https://www.faa.gov/about/office_org/headquarters_offices/ato/service_units/techops/navservices/lsg/papi), NHTSA’s [tire wear bars](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires), GS1’s [barcode check digit](https://www.gs1.org/services/check-digit-calculator), and the U.S. Mint’s [protective coin rim](https://www.usmint.gov/learn/collecting-basics/anatomy-of-a-coin).

Several prior headlines have been narrowed for evidence: the Target spheres brief does not assert crash ratings, the hot-dog brief does not call the item a proven loss leader, and the poultry brief does not claim one facility guarantees a specific price. U.S.-specific rules, vehicle/model exceptions, and variable physical quantities are stated in each brief’s fact boundaries. All briefs retain Tell’s line-drawn guide, mustard scarf, five Gemini stills and five Google Flow animations, and the existing voiced V5 structure.
